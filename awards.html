AWARDS & RECOGNITION

Clinton Destiny

Clinton Destiny, born Cho Clinton Teneng, is an award-winning Cameroonian cultural and gospel musical artist from the Pinyin Clan in Bamenda, North West Region of Cameroon. Recognition includes Gospel music, folklore and cultural music.

Recognition

POMA Award 2020

Recognized as the youngest artist in Pinyin Clan at the time of the award.

PACOMA Award 2021

Recognized as 2nd Best Artist in Gospel Reggae Rhythm.

ATFIMA Award 2022/2023

Received a music trophy and Golden Certificate.

PACOMA Award 2024/2025

Recognized as Best in Folklore.

Heal The World Award 2026

Recognized for Best Cultural Song for UNITY IS STRENGTH featuring Shey Linton YOV.

What the Recognition Represents

These recognitions reflect different parts of Clinton Destiny's work as a Gospel and cultural musical artist. His work brings together gospel music, Afrobeat, Amapiano, Makossa, folklore and community-focused cultural expression.

Recognition has also accompanied his wider involvement in cultural communication, youth engagement and community activities.

Music Connected to Recognition

UNITY IS STRENGTH featuring Shey Linton YOV is among Clinton Destiny's cultural music works and received the Heal The World Award 2026 for Best Cultural Song.
